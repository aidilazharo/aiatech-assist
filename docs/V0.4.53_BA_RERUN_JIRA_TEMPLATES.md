# ATGV 0.4.53 — Business Requirements rerun and Jira template guidance

## Approval and source trust
- Current GATE BRD v2.0 remains a review baseline; its unresolved D-01–D-12 decisions must not be silently treated as resolved.
- TRM_Application_Req.xlsx is a **draft example** of Jira Epics, Stories, subtasks, acceptance criteria and a nine-item DoD. Importing it never approves the listed DoD requirements (including 99% coverage).
- Human approval is required before storing a project-specific DoD policy in project RAG. The default template starts **proposed** and cannot populate Jira DoD until explicitly approved.

## Re-run Business Requirements
1. In Business Analyst > Uploaded Documents, choose **Re-run BR**.
2. Re-run saved source, choose an updated Word/PDF/Excel source, or re-run approved Jira preflight.
3. Source remapping writes no Jira operations. It requires reviewing and approving the new BR revisions.
4. Jira Preview identifies CREATE, UPDATE, RETIRE, and existing unchanged SKIP, preserving issue IDs. Preflight validates existing Jira identity, remote edits, status/history and field mapping.
5. Jira publication still requires explicit BR approval, preview approval and verified publication checkpoints. Interrupted publication must use reconciliation rather than blindly retrying CREATE.

## Template and RAG
- In **Knowledge & RAG**, preview the TRM reference; explicit BA approval stores its version and approval identity under project knowledge.
- Approved Story DoD can be suggested in Jira Preview; proposed acceptance criteria remain based on the selected approved BR and are never silently published.
- Missing Story AC/DoD is a review warning, not a reason to fabricate source requirements. Unapproved policies remain excluded.
- Project RAG indexes project-approved knowledge, but does not imply organization-wide policy or unrestricted reuse across projects.

## Confluence and identity
- My Work roster now stores verified Jira account IDs in an explicit table column to avoid Confluence stripping a data attribute.
- Role refresh checks the signed-in Jira account and may reconcile an explicitly selected role. Jira Assign Issues and candidate assignability remain authoritative.

## Regression focus
- Duplicate AI Review keys and conflicting replacements, Question 12, role registration, old and current source versions, file import, Jira references, template approval and knowledge search.

## Approved template cross-computer sync
The Knowledge & RAG page offers **Sync approved template with Confluence**. This is an explicit BA-confirmed operation. It creates a project-scoped Confluence page on first publish and imports only a verified, project-matching approved template on another computer. Conflicting approved templates halt without overwriting either copy. The project RAG index is refreshed after successful sync.
